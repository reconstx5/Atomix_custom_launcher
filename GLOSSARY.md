# Atomix TV launcher

The TV home screen that replaces the stock launcher: Home's rows, the Apps page, Live TV from the owner's Atomix server,
and Settings.

## Language

**Rows and apps**:
Home's rows, which apps are in each row, and which apps are hidden from Atomix TV, taken as one thing.
_Avoid_: favourites, dock, row store

**Row**:
One line of Home: an app row the owner names and fills, the On now row of live channels, or the Inputs row.
_Avoid_: shelf, section, rail

**Hidden app**:
An installed app the owner has hidden from Atomix TV; it leaves the Apps page and every row but keeps its place in them.
An app that is hidden and no longer installed is not counted as a hidden app.
_Avoid_: removed app, disabled app
